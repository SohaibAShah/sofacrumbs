Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

This policy covers Sofa Crumbs Autopost, a private publishing tool operated by Sofa Crumbs.

Who uses it

Only the owner of the Sofa Crumbs accounts. The tool is not available to the public.

What data it handles

When the owner connects an account (for example with TikTok Login Kit), the tool receives an access token and basic account information (account ID and display name) for that account only. It uses them solely to upload the owner's own videos and captions to that account.

Where data is stored

Tokens are stored only on the owner's computer, in a file readable only by the owner. Nothing is sent to any server other than the platform the video is being posted to. We do not sell, share or analyse any personal data, and we do not collect data about viewers or other users.

Removing access

The owner can revoke access at any time in each platform's settings (on TikTok: Settings and privacy › Security › Manage app permissions) and by deleting the local token file.
